# ♻️ EcoSmart Bharath - AI + IoT Smart Dustbin
### Don't Think. Just Throw. AI Will Sort.

**Team SEC26CS455U | Hackathon 2026**
**Live Repo:** https://github.com/sec26cs455u/ecosmart-bharath-smart-dustbin

## 🚀 Problem
India generates 62M tonnes waste, 70% not segregated. People confused with 4 bins. Bins overflow = mosquitoes + diseases.

## 💡 3-in-1 Solution
### Idea 1 - EcoSort AI (The Brain)
Camera + AI detects waste (plastic bottle). Only correct bin opens (Yellow/Blue/Orange/Green). Others stay CLOSED. Accuracy 94.2%.

### Idea 2 - Smart Bin Alert (The Heart)
Ultrasonic sensor measures fill level. At 75% & 90%, sends GPS alert to collector app. No overflow.

### Idea 3 - Change From Home (The Soul)
QR on bin -> 1-min video "How to segregate at home" in Tamil/Hindi/English. Change starts from home.

## 🏗️ Architecture
**Eyes:** ESP32-CAM
**Brain:** Raspberry Pi + MobileNetV2 AI
**Hands:** 4x Servo Motors
**Mouth:** WiFi + Firebase Alert
**Soul:** Flutter App + Awareness Videos
**Cost:** Rs. 6500-8000

## 🌍 Impact
No Overflow | 90% Less Manual Work | Recycling 30% -> 85% | Clean India
